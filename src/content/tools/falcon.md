Fast Analysis of LTE Control channels — built on srsRAN to blind-decode the entire PDCCH in real time, exposing every DCI/RNTI scheduling grant in a cell. A passive view of live control-channel activity and resource usage. Frozen since 2020 and tied to srsLTE 18.09; LTESniffer, also in this catalogue, is built on FALCON and is the maintained path for the same job.
